GOOD TO KNOW

Before you choose.

Make an informed decision that works for you.

Will a course guarantee me a job or income?

No. A course may help you build knowledge, but income and employment depend on your skills, practice, portfolio, market demand and other factors. Be cautious of guaranteed-income claims.

How do I pay for a course?

Use the enrollment link for the course and review the provider, price, terms and refund policy before you pay. Never share your password, PIN or one-time code with anyone.

What if I cannot afford the listed price?

Open the official course page to see the current price. Compare the course topics and requirements before spending.

Does Earnwise Academy create or teach every course?

Earnwise Academy helps learners discover courses from third-party providers. Course content, access, support and delivery are controlled by each provider.
